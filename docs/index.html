Cosmopedia Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Cosmopedia does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

Data on your device

If you mark spacecraft as favorites, that list is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

No accounts, ads or tracking

The app has no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools.

Internet use

All spacecraft information is stored inside the app. The app works offline. Source links open in your browser, and those websites have their own privacy policies.
